04 / SEVERITY GUIDE & SYMBOLS

Alert legend

ℹ BLUE · INFORMATIONData missing, device disconnected or a condition needing review.
✓ GREEN · NORMALA value is within an explicitly defined training reference range; not proof of health.
⚠ YELLOW · CAUTIONA configured value or scenario warrants closer attention.
◆ ORANGE · HIGH PRIORITYA potentially serious simulated condition calls for prompt assessment.
⛨ RED · CRITICALA critical training condition calls for immediate human-led procedure review.

ACTIVE = newly reported · ACKNOWLEDGED = seen by console operator · UNDER REVIEW = being assessed · RESOLVED = training case closed. These colors and symbols are V.I.T.A.L.S. conventions, not an official NASA clinical severity scale.
