05 / PROVENANCE & INTERPRETATION

Understanding these readings

SIMULATED

Numbers are produced by training algorithms in the device pages, not by a physical wearable.

RECENT ≠ VERIFIED LIVE SENSOR

“Recent synthetic activity” means new browser-stored demo observations. A device being ON does not prove measurement.

PERSONAL DATA SEPARATION

Readings are filtered by mission and astronaut ID, and are not mixed with another crew member’s values.
