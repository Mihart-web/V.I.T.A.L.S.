03 — RADIATION SYSTEMS

Radiation Assessment

GCR / SEP / TRAPPED PARTICLES

Radiation types

GCR: persistent galactic cosmic rays; SEP: intermittent solar energetic particles; trapped particles: environment-dependent, notably in planetary magnetospheres.

The presence of radiation does not determine an astronaut's dose without a detector, shielding and a defined exposure history.

Detection & protection

Personal dosimetry, area monitors and particle detectors can provide different measurements. Instrument readings must retain quantity, units, location and calibration context.

Radiation protection follows the ALARA principle. EVA decisions and shelter actions require operational procedures and authorized personnel.
