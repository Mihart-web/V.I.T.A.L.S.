Floodwater, precipitation and disrupted logistics

A floodplain response mission depends on rainfall, watershed, terrain, hydrology and changing local forecasts. Satellite precipitation estimates are not ground-truth water-depth readings.

This is a scenario-level reference. No local observation, health diagnosis, alert, or forecast is generated.

OPERATIONS / SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Risk & Crew Considerations

01 / RAIN RATE

Location / time

IMERG satellite estimates. Satellite rainfall estimates are a monitoring input, not a flood-depth sensor.

02 / WATER DEPTH

Not assigned

Requires on-site measurements. Fast-moving water and submerged obstacles can be dangerous.

03 / CURRENT VELOCITY

Site-dependent

Moving water is hazardous. Water contamination and electrical hazards need separate evaluation.

04 / CONTAMINATION

Possible

Water quality not verified. No live flood forecast or automated rescue routing is provided.

05 / ACCESSIBILITY

Disrupted

Road / bridge closure risk. Satellite rainfall estimates are a monitoring input, not a flood-depth sensor.

06 / CREW HAZARD

Injury / exposure

Operational fatigue and infection risk. Fast-moving water and submerged obstacles can be dangerous.
