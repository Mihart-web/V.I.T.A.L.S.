Ash, gases and hazardous access corridors

Volcanic exposure is event-, wind- and distance-dependent. No fixed plume concentration, lava temperature at a site or gas dose is assigned.

This is a scenario-level reference. No local observation, health diagnosis, alert, or forecast is generated.

OPERATIONS / SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Risk & Crew Considerations

01 / VOLCANIC ASH

Variable loading

Abrasive fine particles. Fine ash is abrasive and can disrupt mechanical equipment and engines.

02 / SO₂ / CO₂

Monitor locally

Gas concentrations event-specific. SO₂, CO₂ and other volcanic gases vary with volcanic system and weather.

03 / VISIBILITY

Ash dependent

Wind / plume orientation. Protective equipment must be based on measured concentrations.

04 / AIR QUALITY

Potentially hazardous

Respiratory protection may be needed. Evacuation corridors can be affected by ash and lahars.

05 / AVIATION

Ash hazard

Air traffic can be disrupted. Fine ash is abrasive and can disrupt mechanical equipment and engines.

06 / THERMAL HAZARD

Proximity-dependent

Hot materials and terrain. SO₂, CO₂ and other volcanic gases vary with volcanic system and weather.
