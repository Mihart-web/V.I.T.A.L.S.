PLANETARY OVERVIEW

A World of Methane Seas and Organic Haze

Titan is Saturn's largest moon. Its dense, nitrogen-rich atmosphere conceals a landscape shaped by methane rainfall, rivers, lakes and seas of hydrocarbons. It is extremely cold, and evidence from Cassini suggests a subsurface ocean may exist under the icy crust.

TITAN ENVIRONMENT / ATMOSPHERE

A Dense Nitrogen Atmosphere

Titan's atmosphere is primarily nitrogen with methane and trace organic compounds. Sunlight and energetic particles drive its chemical reactions, producing a characteristic haze. Surface pressure is roughly 1.5 bar, but the atmosphere is not breathable.

TITAN ENVIRONMENT / SURFACE

Methane Seas and an Icy Landscape

Titan has rivers, lakes and seas containing liquid hydrocarbons, especially methane and ethane. Water ice behaves as a rock-like material at Titan's extremely low surface temperatures. Cassini data also support the possibility of a subsurface liquid-water ocean.
