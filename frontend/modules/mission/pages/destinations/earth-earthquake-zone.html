Shaking, aftershocks and unstable infrastructure

No fixed quake magnitude or ground acceleration is assigned. Earthquake intensity and secondary hazards change with event, site geology and building conditions.

This is a scenario-level reference. No local observation, health diagnosis, alert, or forecast is generated.

OPERATIONS / SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Risk & Crew Considerations

01 / SHAKING INTENSITY

Event-specific

USGS ShakeMap input. Shaking intensity is not the same as earthquake magnitude.

02 / AFTERSHOCKS

Possible

Time-varying event sequence. Weak sediments can amplify local ground motion.

03 / GROUND FAILURE

Site-dependent

Landslides and liquefaction. Liquefaction requires susceptible water-saturated soils.

04 / STRUCTURE STATUS

Unverified

Requires engineer assessment. Assessment and rescue actions must follow actual site safety protocols.

05 / ACCESS ROUTES

May be blocked

Debris and damaged bridges. Shaking intensity is not the same as earthquake magnitude.

06 / CREW HAZARD

Trauma / rescue

Exposure and operational stress. Weak sediments can amplify local ground motion.
