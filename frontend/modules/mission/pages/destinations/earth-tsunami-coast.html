Inundation, currents and delayed wave trains

A tsunami-prone coast has no universal wave height or arrival time. A tsunami can consist of multiple waves and conditions depend on the source and coastal geometry.

This is a scenario-level reference. No local observation, health diagnosis, alert, or forecast is generated.

OPERATIONS / SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Risk & Crew Considerations

01 / ARRIVAL TIME

Source-dependent

No universal forecast. Local sources can leave very little time for official alerts.

02 / WAVE HEIGHT

Coast-dependent

Bathymetry and run-up vary. Multiple waves may continue for hours; never treat the first wave as the all-clear.

03 / INUNDATION

Mapped locally

Topography controls exposure. Run-up and flooding differ by coastline and bathymetry.

04 / WAVE TRAIN

Multiple waves

Do not assume first is largest. This prototype does not issue tsunami warnings or route evacuations.

05 / CURRENT HAZARD

Strong / variable

Ports and channels vulnerable. Local sources can leave very little time for official alerts.

06 / EVACUATION

High ground

Use local official warnings. Multiple waves may continue for hours; never treat the first wave as the all-clear.
