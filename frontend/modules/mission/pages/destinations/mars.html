PLANETARY OVERVIEW

Exploring the Martian Environment

Mars is a cold, rocky desert with a thin carbon dioxide-rich atmosphere. Its dust, temperature extremes, low pressure and space radiation present challenges for human exploration. The values below are planetary references or historical measurements—not live readings for a specific landing site.
