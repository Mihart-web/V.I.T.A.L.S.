PLANETARY OVERVIEW

An Ocean World Beneath the Ice

Europa, a moon of Jupiter, has an icy surface crossed by fractures, ridges and bands. Multiple observations support the existence of a global salty ocean beneath its crust. Its extremely thin oxygen exosphere is not breathable, and energetic particles from Jupiter create an intense radiation environment.

RADIATION ENVIRONMENT

Jupiter's Radiation Belts

Europa orbits within Jupiter's powerful magnetosphere. Energetic charged particles present a significant challenge to unshielded surface operations. Astronaut dose would depend on exposure time, local particle flux, shielding and mission design; this page does not assign a person-specific radiation dose.

EUROPA ENVIRONMENT / OCEAN

The Ocean Beneath the Ice

Magnetic and geological measurements strongly support a subsurface saltwater ocean. Water, chemical ingredients and potential energy sources make Europa scientifically interesting for habitability studies. No life has been detected there.
